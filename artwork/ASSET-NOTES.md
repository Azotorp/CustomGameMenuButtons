# Supplied artwork

The owner selected cyan sliders for main, green sigil for ForeverAuras and red arrow for Reload UI. Masters are exact supplied PNG bytes; checksums and source dimensions are in manifest.json. Downsampling preserves the full source without cropping or stretching and centers transparent padding where needed. No recolor or redraw is performed.
